# BreakGuard V0.1 — proposed pilot offer

**Working draft updated October 26, 2026, for Oleg's approval. The current
proposal is GBP 42.00. An earlier feedback email mentioned EUR 49; that historical
reference is not a fixed exchange rate or the current invoice currency. This
complete paid offer has not been sent or accepted.**

## Proposed customer-facing wording

BreakGuard is an early local developer tool for three known Shopify deprecation
families: Customer Account `Customer.lastIncompleteCheckout`, Admin
`DraftOrderDiscountNotAppliedWarning.priceRule`, and ScriptTag create/update
patterns. It locates source candidates with official guidance and previews
guarded edits for supported standalone GraphQL cases. Static embedded GraphQL
and ScriptTag findings remain manual.

The proposed pilot covers one developer/team and one repository, up to 1,000
eligible source files, using the agreed build and its current rules. It includes
the installable package, local scan/coverage report, preview/apply/restore flow,
onboarding instructions and up to two asynchronous support exchanges over seven
calendar days from the agreed start. Delivery/start dates are agreed before
payment. This scope and support commitment require the Founder's approval.

No store credentials, private code submission or store access are required.
The developer runs the tool locally and reviews changes and application tests.
This is not a general REST-to-GraphQL migration, API compatibility certification,
ongoing monitoring subscription or guarantee that a Shopify warning disappears.

## Free evaluation and the proposed paid scope

The free evaluation kit remains free. It provides the working candidate and
synthetic examples; no payment or purchase commitment is needed for that test.
The proposed paid pilot adds the agreed assistance for one actual eligible
repository, the bounded support below and the customer-specific delivery record.
Do not imply that the paid pilot unlocks additional migration rules or a broader
automatic fixer. Installation feedback alone does not establish buyer fit.

## Price proposal and rationale

Before presenting this complete offer, follow `PILOT_ELIGIBILITY.md`. A migration
issue mentioning one of the field names does not establish that the developer's
actual project contains a useful supported case. A no-findings control may help
technical feedback, but is not evidence of a qualified buyer.

**GBP 42.00, one-time proposed pilot total — commercial usefulness and willingness
to pay remain unvalidated.**

The invoice is denominated in GBP. A buyer may use another supported payment
currency, with conversion at PayPal's or the card issuer's applicable rate and
charges. GBP 42 is not guaranteed to equal EUR 49. Do not display a fixed euro
equivalent or promise that every currency/payment method is available.

- The current scope is narrow and bounded, so a one-time pilot is easier to test
  than a subscription with an unproven recurring benefit.
- The fee tests whether supported source findings, guidance and assistance have
  value beyond existing IDE searches and platform/schema tools. It is not a
  measured market price, cost calculation or revenue forecast.
- Shopify already shows deprecated API alerts. GraphQL Inspector can validate
  documents against a schema and report deprecated usage. BreakGuard must earn
  its price through its particular local workflow and useful Shopify guidance;
  novelty, superiority and willingness to pay are not established.
- Confirm tax treatment and the final GBP total for the actual seller/customer
  before sending an invoice. The working proposal is a GBP 42.00 customer total;
  if it cannot cover applicable taxes, obtain a new approved and accepted quote
  before charging. Do not add an undisclosed amount after acceptance.
- The separate Sandbox module also uses GBP 42.00 as test data. Its offline fake
  is not a live receipt or an actual Sandbox account transaction.

The first validation asks about a recent concrete migration, useful/missing
results and willingness to accept this exact bounded offer. Record a declined
offer honestly. If the core cases do not matter to the developer, revise the
scope using evidence before expanding features or charging.

## Proposed service recovery

Proposed policy, not yet approved: accept a full GBP 42.00 refund request for
any reason within seven calendar days from the agreed pilot start. Also offer
a full refund if the agreed delivery cannot be provided by its written deadline.
The Founder would initiate an approved refund through the original transaction
within five business days; bank/provider settlement times and currency conversion
outcomes are outside that promise. No refund is executed by this draft.

Confirm that this policy is feasible for the actual account before offering it.
Do not assume processing/conversion fees are returned or deduct them silently
from a promised full refund. Mandatory customer/provider rights are unchanged.
Delivery, support and license proposals are detailed in `PILOT_TERMS_DRAFT.md`;
use `PILOT_ORDER_TEMPLATE.md` and `PAID_PILOT_RUNBOOK.md` before any paid delivery.
