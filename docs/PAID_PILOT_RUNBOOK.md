# First paid pilot — proposed manual runbook

Status: working procedure for Founder approval, October 26, 2026. No invoice,
message, payment, customer agreement or delivery is created by this file. This
manual route does not require claiming the separate API adapter is production
ready. Every external action remains subject to the Founder's authorization.

## 1. Confirm fit before asking for payment

Use `PILOT_ELIGIBILITY.md`. A consenting Shopify developer should identify a
current relevant occurrence, actual API/version and supported source form, and
say what useful action the local report changes. A free sample install or an
unfilled demo-request email is not evidence of a qualified paid buyer.

Keep free evaluation free. The proposed GBP 42 pilot buys the bounded agreed
assistance for an actual eligible repository, not extra hidden migration rules.
If there is no useful supported case, keep that as technical feedback and do not
promise a solution or ask for payment on that basis.

## 2. Complete one private order before invoicing

Fill `PILOT_ORDER_TEMPLATE.md` in a private record, outside this public repository.
Confirm seller identity, lawful activity/tax route, the actual account's ability
to receive commercial payments and perform refunds, buyer business status and
jurisdiction, the pinned build, deadline, support dates, final GBP total, license
and refund terms. Do not guess missing seller/customer information. The offer
and the proposed seven-day refund policy need the Founder's approval.

Obtain the buyer's explicit acceptance of the complete order. Record price
interest, accepted offer and verified payment separately. Do not ask for private
source, passwords, API tokens, store credentials or a backup archive.

## 3. Use a separate hosted invoice for the actual buyer

After those approvals, the owner can create one provider-hosted invoice for that
customer/transaction: correct seller, buyer, title `BreakGuard — Pilot`, agreed
scope/reference, GBP currency and the agreed final total (currently proposed
GBP 42.00). No recurring charge, hidden extra or personal-transfer classification.

A single invoice is not a reusable website checkout. A personal QR code is not
proof of an online product checkout. Keep the current presentation page without
an active invoice/payment button until its separate publication/payment approval.
Neither the owner-created invoice nor this runbook activates the Python adapter.

## 4. Verify the receipt in the provider account

The owner verifies the actual transaction in their own PayPal activity: the
correct seller, buyer/order reference, amount, GBP currency and final payment
status. A buyer email, screenshot, browser return or fake receipt is insufficient.
If pending, cancelled, mismatched, refunded or uncertain, record the exact status
and resolve it before treating the pilot as paid. If a completed payment has a
hold, record payment completion and fund availability separately; the Founder
reviews provider instructions before deciding delivery. Do not request a second
payment to release a hold. A completed payment and immediately withdrawable funds
are not the same statement.

Never send secrets through chat. No automatic capture, account change or refund
is performed by BreakGuard's source scanner or this document.

## 5. Deliver only the agreed package

Verify its SHA-256 and manifest; retain the pinned runtime identity. The agreed
package should include instructions, synthetic examples, manual/dynamic controls
and the feedback record, without seller intake or other customers' information.
Gmail blocks `.cmd` and `.js` even inside ZIP archives. Use a private Drive file
shared read-only with the exact buyer email, rather than renaming files or
password-protecting an archive. Verify the reader permission before sending.

After approval, send one delivery email with the link, checksum, start/end dates
and instructions to use a disposable copy and review preview/apply/restore. Log
the sent message separately from buyer receipt, download or completed testing;
none implies the others. Preserve the original private web demo's audience.

## 6. Support, outcome and refunds

Start the proposed seven calendar days and two asynchronous exchanges on the
explicit agreed date. Help with installation, supported CLI behavior, redacted
findings and restore. Do not promise application-level safety, a new migration
rule, a live store intervention or continuous availability.

Record the developer's actual assessment and app-test outcome; do not infer
commercial usefulness from a passing synthetic demo. A negative result is useful
evidence and may justify stopping or changing scope.

For an approved refund request, the owner uses the original provider transaction,
checks any existing partial/full refund first, and records the amount/status.
A full proposed GBP 42 refund is not a guaranteed EUR equivalent; provider/bank
conversion and posting times can differ. The proposal is to initiate a refund
within five business days, not to guarantee bank settlement in that time. Do not
invent a refund confirmation or execute an expense without authorization.
