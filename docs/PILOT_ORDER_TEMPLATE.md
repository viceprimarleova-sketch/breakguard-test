## Proposed scope for the completed order

One developer/team and one repository, up to 1,000 eligible files, with the
current three rule families and bounded assistance. Includes the agreed package,
local reports and preview/apply/restore instructions, plus up to two asynchronous
email exchanges over seven calendar days from the stated start. Application
review and tests remain with the developer; embedded and ScriptTag findings stay
manual. No general Shopify/Hydrogen migration, store access or compatibility
guarantee is included. The free sample kit is not retroactively charged.

Proposed use grant: the pinned build for the one agreed repository, without an
automatic expiry; no future upgrades, subscriptions, resale or new rules are
included. Final ownership/license review and dependency rights still apply.

Proposed refund policy: the full GBP 42.00 on request within seven calendar days
from the agreed start, or if the agreed delivery deadline cannot be met. The
seller would initiate an approved refund through the original payment within
five business days. Actual provider/bank posting and currency conversion are
outside that timing promise. Mandatory rights are unchanged. This policy must
be approved and feasible in the actual account before it is offered.
